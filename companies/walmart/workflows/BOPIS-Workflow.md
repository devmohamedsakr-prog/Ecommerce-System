# Walmart: BOPIS Workflow (Buy Online Pickup In Store)

## Customer Journey

### 1. Browse & Purchase
- Customer searches Walmart.com
- Checks store availability (auto-locates nearest)
- Adds to cart, selects BOPIS
- Pays online (secure checkout)

### 2. Order Confirmation
- Order confirmed immediately
- Email confirmation sent
- Estimated pickup: 1-3 hours
- Pickup instructions emailed

### 3. Store Fulfillment
- Fulfillment alert sent to nearest store
- Store staff notified via mobile app
- Team picks item from shelves
- Packing: Into Walmart bag (labeled)
- Location: Held at customer service desk

### 4. Customer Notification
- Text sent: "Your order ready for pickup"
- Email sent: Pickup instructions
- Notification includes: Order number, pickup time window
- Optional: Curbside delivery (no store entry)

### 5. Pickup
- Customer arrives at store
- Check-in at customer service
- Staff retrieves order
- Payment: Already processed
- Pickup: Seconds

### 6. Curbside Option
- If customer doesn't want to enter
- Staff brings order to car
- No contact pickup available
- Especially useful during COVID

## Operational Benefits
- Store traffic: Increased (BOPIS customer often shops)
- Cross-selling: 40% of BOPIS customers buy additional items
- Fulfillment cost: $3-5 per order (vs $8-12 shipping)
- Return rate: Lower than shipped orders

## Customer Benefits
- Speed: 1-3 hours vs 2-day delivery
- Convenience: Pick up near home
- Certainty: See item in person
- Flexibility: Pickup window (8am-8pm)

## Scale
- BOPIS orders: 50M+ annually (2026)
- Penetration: 30% of Walmart ecommerce
- Growth: 20%+ YoY
- Future: Target 50%+ of orders
