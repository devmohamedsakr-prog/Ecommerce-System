# Walmart: Omnichannel Integration Architecture

## Store Network Asset
- 4500+ stores (USA + International)
- Strategic advantage: Geographic density
- Mini-fulfillment centers: Each store
- Customer proximity: 90% population within 15 miles

## Fulfillment Models

### BOPIS (Buy Online Pickup In Store)
- 30% of Walmart+ ecommerce orders
- Fulfillment: From nearby store
- Time: 1-3 hours (vs 2-day delivery)
- Customer benefit: Immediate gratification
- Walmart benefit: Reduced shipping cost

### Ship-from-Store
- 40% of digital orders
- Fulfillment: From nearby store inventory
- vs. Fulfillment center (often distant)
- Shipping: Faster (2-day standard)
- Cost: Lower (local shipping, no FC overhead)

### Reserve-In-Store
- Digital reserve, in-store trial
- Fashion/shoes try-on capability
- Then purchase or ship
- Customer benefit: Try before buy
- Walmart benefit: Higher conversion

## Unified Inventory System
- Visibility: Every store stock level (real-time)
- Dynamic routing: Order routes to nearest store
- Allocation: Sells vs hold inventory (algorithm)
- Stockout prediction: Reorder before empty
- Result: 95%+ fulfillment accuracy

## Infrastructure
- POS systems: Integrated with ecommerce
- Fulfillment apps: Store staff pick/pack digital orders
- Scanning: RFID tracking of high-value items
- Shipping: Integration with major carriers
- Returns: In-store or mail (customer choice)

## Competitive Advantage
- Amazon: 200+ fulfillment centers
- Walmart: 4500+ mini fulfillment centers
- Result: Walmart faster for local delivery
