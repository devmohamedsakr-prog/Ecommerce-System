# Walmart Scalability: Store-Based Fulfillment Network

## Store Network as Advantage
- 4500+ stores (USA)
- Geographic coverage: 90% population <15 miles
- vs Amazon fulfillment centers: 200+ (less dense)
- Result: Walmart potentially faster for local delivery

## Fulfillment Cost Advantage
- BOPIS: $3-5 per order (pickup, no shipping)
- Ship-from-store: $6-8 per order (local courier)
- Traditional fulfillment: $10-15 per order (long distance)
- Amazon: Average $8-12 per order (mixed fulfillment)
- Walmart advantage: 30-50% lower cost per order

## Capacity Calculation
- Per-store capacity: 100-200 BOPIS + ship-from-store orders daily
- 4500 stores: 450K-900K orders daily potential
- Current utilization: 20% (underutilized)
- Growth room: 4x before needing new stores

## Infrastructure
- Mobile apps: Store staff pick orders
- Sorting: Bagging area (separate from retail)
- Staging: Hold area (1-3 hour window)
- Shipping: Integration with major carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS)
- Returns: In-store processing (free)

## Peak Season Management
- Black Friday: 3x normal order volume
- Store prep: Additional staff training
- Inventory pre-positioning: Stock high-velocity items
- Extended hours: Pickup until 10pm (vs normal 6pm)

## Profitability Analysis
- Fulfillment cost: $6-8 (lower than Amazon)
- Fulfillment time: 1-3 hours (faster than Amazon)
- Conversion: Higher (visible, nearby option)
- Margin: Better than shipped orders (lower customer acquisition friction)

## Limitations
- SKU selection: Not all items ship-from-store
- Perishables: Fresh groceries complex to ship
- High-value: Electronics limited availability
- Solution: Omnichannel (combine models)

## Future Scalability
- Same-day: Expand to 5000+ areas (currently 2000)
- Autonomous: Store-to-car delivery (test underway)
- Drones: Last-mile from stores (experimental)
- Result: Sustained competitive advantage
