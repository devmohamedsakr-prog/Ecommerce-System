# Rakuten Scalability: Limited Global, Focused Japan

## Market Strategy
- Japan: Core market (70% business)
- Asia selective: Korea (10%), Taiwan (5%)
- Global: Limited presence (15%)
- Rationale: Ecosystem works at home, harder globally

## Japan Operations Scale
- Users: 100M+ (80%+ penetration)
- GMV: $100B+ annually
- Services: 8+ integrated businesses
- Points: 5T+ annually circulating
- Infrastructure: Highly optimized
- Cost: Lowest per-transaction in industry

## Asia Selective Expansion
- South Korea: Gmarket (purchased 2010)
  - Market: $50B+ ecommerce market
  - Position: #2-3 in Korea
  - Points: Adapted to Korean market
  - Status: Profitable but slower growth than Japan

- Taiwan: Limited presence
  - Market: $30B+ ecommerce
  - Entry: Via Rakuten Ichiba Taiwan
  - Status: Small player (not #1)

## Global Presence
- USA: Divested (failed expansion)
- Europe: Minimal (no major presence)
- Southeast Asia: Limited (Shopee, Lazada dominate)
- Rationale: Ecosystem model doesn't scale globally

## Infrastructure Design
- Japan: Deep investment (scale, optimization)
- Asia: Moderate investment (profitable but not dominant)
- Global: Light investment (not priority)
- Result: Capital efficiency, focused growth

## Financial Discipline
- ROIC target: 15%+ (achieved in Japan)
- Global expansion halted: Pursuing lower returns
- Capital allocation: To high-return Japan market
- Result: Higher profitability vs pursuing global scale

## Growth Outlook
- Japan: Mature market (2-3% annual growth)
- Asia: Modest growth (8-12% annual)
- Global: Likely to remain small (not strategic priority)
- Total: $150B+ GMV by 2030 (from $100B+ current)
