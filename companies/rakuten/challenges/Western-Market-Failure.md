# Rakuten Challenge: Western Market Entry Failure

## Market Entry Strategy
- Rakuten.com (USA): Launched 2010
- Goal: Replicate Japan success in USA
- Strategy: Same ecosystem model
- Investment: $100M+ in USA expansion

## Why It Failed
1. **Points System Not Compelling:**
   - US shoppers value convenience over points
   - Points = 1% value (vs 5-15% in Japan)
   - Amazon Prime membership > points rewards

2. **Amazon Entrenched:**
   - Amazon Prime: 150M+ members in USA
   - Same-day delivery: Hard to compete
   - Brand dominance: Rakuten not recognized

3. **Ecosystem Doesn't Translate:**
   - Banking: Not primary revenue driver in US
   - Credit card: Visa/Mastercard commoditized
   - Insurance: Separate market (not bundled)

4. **Late Entry:**
   - eBay: 20+ years established
   - Amazon: 15+ years established
   - Network effects: Hard to overcome

## Outcomes
- Rakuten.com: Shut down 2013 (after losses)
- USA marketplace: Divested to local players
- Investment: $100M+ loss
- Lesson: Ecosystem model works in home market, not global

## Lessons Learned
1. **Home market advantage matters:** Japan = 80% of business
2. **Ecosystem requires critical mass:** Hard to start from zero
3. **Global expansion risky:** Stick to strengths
4. **Market dynamics differ:** Points valuable in Japan, not USA

## Strategic Shift
- Post-failure: Focus on Asia selective expansion
- Japan: 70% of business (maintained dominance)
- South Korea: Partial entry (smaller market)
- Taiwan: Small presence
- Global: Accept Rakuten #4-5 player (not #1)
