# Rakuten: Points Lifecycle Workflow

## Earn Phase
1. **Shopping:** Buy on Ichiba (1-15% earn rate)
2. **Payments:** Use Rakuten Card (1% earn rate)
3. **Banking:** Set up Rakuten Bank (bonus points)
4. **Travel:** Book on Rakuten Travel (2-10% earn rate)
5. **Other:** Mobile plan, insurance, investments

## Accumulation Management
- Annual circulation: 5T+ points (5 trillion)
- Average user balance: 100-500 points (highly variable)
- Expiration: 12 months (encourages redemption)
- Special promotions: 2x/5x earn events (monthly)

## Redemption Options
1. **Cash Back:** 1 point = 1 yen (~0.007 USD)
2. **Shopping:** Use in Ichiba checkout
3. **Travel:** Discount on bookings
4. **Donations:** Charity programs
5. **Gift Cards:** Partner retailers
6. **Investments:** Purchase investment products

## Points Transfer Workflow
1. Initiate transfer
2. Send to family member (email)
3. Recipient accepts
4. Points move to recipient account
5. Usage tracked separately

## Business Intelligence
- Earn patterns: Identify high-spending segments
- Redemption patterns: Which services generate most value
- Churn prediction: Point balance declining = churn signal
- Lifetime value: By earn/redeem ratio

## Loyalty Mechanics
- Spend $1000/year → VIP tier
- VIP → Higher earn rate (15% vs 5%)
- Exclusive offers → VIP only
- Result: Tier-based incentivizes spending

## System Scale
- Daily earn transactions: 10M+
- Quarterly redemptions: 2-3T points
- Database: Central points ledger (critical system)
- Peak: Black Friday (50x normal earn rate)
