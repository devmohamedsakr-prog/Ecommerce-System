# Shopify Case Study: Platform Growth to 1M+ Merchants

## Growth Timeline
- 2006: Founded (small team, custom stores)
- 2010: 10K+ merchants (scaling begins)
- 2015: 250K+ merchants (IPO, IPO price $17/share)
- 2020: 1M+ merchants (COVID boost, SME shift online)
- 2026: 1.5M+ merchants (projected)

## Merchant Diversity
- Handmade: Etsy competitor (30%)
- Small business: Local stores going digital (30%)
- SME: Growing companies (20%)
- Enterprise: Large retailers (10%)
- Creator economy: Social sellers (10%)

## GMV Growth
- 2010: $100M GMV
- 2015: $10B GMV (100x)
- 2020: $300B+ GMV (30x)
- 2026: $600B+ GMV (2x projected)

## Revenue Model
- Transaction fee: 2.9% + $0.30 (primary)
- Subscription: $29-299/month (base)
- App store: 30% commission (growing)
- Shopify Payments: 2.9% + $0.30 (unified gateway)

## Annual Revenue
- 2020: $3B (transaction + subscription)
- 2023: $5B+ (growing faster)
- 2026: $8B+ (forecast)

## Competitive Position
- vs WooCommerce: Simpler (no self-hosting)
- vs BigCommerce: Similar but Shopify larger
- vs custom: Faster to market (no dev needed)
- vs Magento: Too complex for SMEs
- Result: Market leader (35-40% market share)

## Success Factors
1. **Low friction:** 1M+ merchants launched (low barrier)
2. **Scalable:** Works for $10K to $10M merchants
3. **App ecosystem:** 3000+ apps (extend functionality)
4. **Community:** Forums, courses, support
5. **Payments:** Integrated Shopify Payments (no friction)

## Financial Performance
- Stock price: $17 (2015) → $80+ (2026)
- Market cap: $50B+ (2026)
- Profitability: Path to profitability (2024)
- Margins: Improving (economies of scale)

## International Expansion
- 175+ countries supported
- Multi-currency: 100+ currencies
- Localized: Language, payment, tax
- Result: Shopify truly global platform
