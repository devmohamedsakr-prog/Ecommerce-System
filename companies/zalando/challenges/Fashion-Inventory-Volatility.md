# Zalando Challenge: Fashion Inventory Volatility

## Problem
- Fashion trends change weekly
- Forecasting error: 20-40% common
- Overstock: Become clearance (20-50% loss)
- Understock: Lost sales (opportunity cost)
- Size mix volatility: Which sizes sell varies by trend

## Solution 1: ML Demand Forecasting
- Historical data: 10 years of sales
- Trend indicators: Social media, influencer mentions
- Weather data: Coat demand changes with weather
- Competitor data: Price/trends tracked
- Result: Accuracy improved to 85%+

## Solution 2: Flexible Supplier Relationships
- Long-term contracts: Adjusted quarterly
- Fast fashion: Rush orders (higher cost, lower MOQ)
- Bulk: Standard lead time (better price)
- Mix: Balanced approach (70% bulk, 30% fast)

## Solution 3: Dynamic Pricing
- Demand curve: Real-time pricing adjustments
- Overstock: Automatic discounts (clear inventory)
- Understock: Price premium (limit purchases)
- Seasonal: Predictive seasonal pricing
- Result: Inventory turns improved 40%+

## Solution 4: Returns Strategy
- Easy returns: 100-day window
- Re-listing: 70% of returns resold
- Clearance: 20% discounted
- Liquidation: 10% auction/outlet
- Result: Recover value from mistakes

## Inventory Management
- SKU count: 2M+ active
- Warehouse: 20+ Europe locations
- Inventory churn: Every 4-6 weeks (fast)
- Dead stock: <5% (vs industry 10-15%)

## Financial Impact
- Carrying cost: Reduced 20% (faster turns)
- Markdown rate: 5-7% (vs industry 10-15%)
- Profitability: Improved 2-3 percentage points
- Customer satisfaction: Better availability
