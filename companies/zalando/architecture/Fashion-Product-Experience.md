# Zalando: Fashion Product Experience Architecture

## Virtual Try-On Technology
- AR sizing: Use device camera
- Measure customer: Body measurements
- Recommend size: Per-brand size chart
- Accuracy: 95%+ correct size prediction
- Result: Reduced returns (30-50% industry norm)

## Size Guide System
- Crowdsourced: 500K+ user reviews per season
- Brand-specific: Different brands fit different
- Measurement data: Height, weight, size fit
- ML model: Predict best size for customer
- Updated: Weekly (based on customer feedback)

## Product Imagery
- 360° views: See item from all angles
- Video: Model wearing (different angles)
- Fit video: Same size/style on models (height comparison)
- Color accuracy: Calibrated photos (true color)
- Result: More confident purchase decisions

## Easy Returns
- Return window: 100 days (vs industry 30-60)
- Free returns: No restocking fee
- Process: Drop off at Zalando station or home pickup
- Refund: 5-10 days after receipt
- Result: Removes purchase friction (customer pain)

## AI Recommendations
- Item-to-item: "Similar items"
- User-to-item: "Based on your style"
- Personalization: ML model (browsing history)
- Trending: Trending now section (real-time)
- Result: 35%+ of revenue from recommendations

## Sustainability Tracking
- Carbon footprint: Displayed per item
- Shipping method: Standard vs express impact
- Material: Sustainable fabric indicators
- Result: Appeals to conscious consumers
