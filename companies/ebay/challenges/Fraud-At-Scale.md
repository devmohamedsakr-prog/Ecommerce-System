# eBay Challenge: Fraud Detection at 30M+ Seller Scale

## Problem
- Counterfeit items (INAD - item not as described)
- Non-delivery (seller takes payment, doesn't ship)
- Account hijacking (stolen credentials)
- Chargeback fraud (buyer claims no delivery)

## Solutions
1. **Seller Verification:** ID check, payment method verification
2. **Buyer Protection:** Full refund if issues
3. **AI Detection:** Pattern recognition for fraud
4. **Managed Payments:** 21-day hold on seller funds
5. **Escalation:** Manual review for high-value items
6. **Suspension:** Repeat offenders removed immediately

## Results
- 99.7% transaction success rate
- <0.3% fraud rate (vs industry 1-2%)
- Buyer confidence: 95%+ trust in eBay protection
