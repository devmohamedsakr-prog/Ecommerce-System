# Mercado Libre: Mercado Pago as Core Architecture

## Platform Value Distribution
- Marketplace commission: 30% of profit
- Mercado Pago: 70% of profit (payments service)
- Mercado Crédito: Growing (financing for sellers)

## Mercado Pago Scale
- Users: 35M+ active
- Annual volume: $250B+ (higher than marketplace)
- Penetration: Available in all 18 countries

## Architecture
1. **Payment Processing:**
   - Card processing (Visa, Mastercard)
   - Bank transfers (growing adoption)
   - Digital wallets (local)
   - Crypto experiments (Bitcoin, USDC)

2. **Risk Management:**
   - ML fraud detection
   - Device fingerprinting
   - Velocity checks
   - Chargeback management

3. **Settlement:**
   - T+0 for marketplace orders (immediate)
   - T+1 for standalone payments
   - Seller payout: Daily available (fee applies)

## Regional Payment Methods
- **Brazil:** Pix (instant, 50%+ of payments)
- **Mexico:** OXXO cash payment
- **Colombia:** Local bank transfers
- **Argentina:** Bank transfer, cash
- **Chile:** Bank transfer, local cards

## Integration
- Seller financing: Mercado Crédito
- Buyer credit: Installment plans
- Cross-border: Unified payment across regions
- Result: Customers don't leave platform for payments

## Competitive Advantage
- Vertical integration (vs external Stripe)
- Lower fees for sellers (vs Stripe/Square)
- Local payment methods (vs international-only)
- Seller financing (enables growth)
