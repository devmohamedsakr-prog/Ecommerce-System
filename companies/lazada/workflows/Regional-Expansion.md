# Lazada Regional Expansion Workflow

## Phase 1: Market Research (2-3 months)
- Population demographics
- ecommerce penetration
- Competitor analysis (Shopee, Amazon)
- Payment methods available
- Regulatory requirements

## Phase 2: Local Partnerships (2 months)
- Courier partnerships (3-5 major couriers)
- Bank partnerships (payment processing)
- Logistics provider selection
- Regulatory/legal setup

## Phase 3: Seller Recruitment (3 months)
- Seller outreach (SMEs in region)
- Seller training (platform features)
- Incentive programs (reduced fees initial)
- Inventory stocking (encourage bulk)

## Phase 4: Marketing Campaign (2 months)
- TV commercials
- Social media blitz
- Influencer partnerships
- Launch event marketing

## Phase 5: Platform Launch (Launch day)
- Soft launch (100K users)
- Real-time monitoring
- Incident response team ready
- Customer support in local language

## Phase 6: Optimization (6 months post-launch)
- Payment method localization
- Logistics optimization
- Seller incentive tuning
- Marketing campaign refining

## Timeline
Total: ~12 months from market selection to scaled operation
