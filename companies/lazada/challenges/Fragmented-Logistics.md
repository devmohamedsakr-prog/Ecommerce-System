# Lazada Challenge: Fragmented Regional Logistics

## Problem
Each SE Asia country has different:
- Courier companies (no global player)
- Infrastructure quality (cities vs rural)
- Regulations (import duties, customs)
- Costs (island delivery 3-5x more expensive)
- Timelines (2-day urban, 7+ day rural)

## Solution: Hybrid Logistics Model
1. **LazLog In-House:** Handle 30% of volume
   - Metro areas only (cost-effective)
   - Own fleet: Faster, controllable
   - Direct relationships with merchants

2. **Partner Couriers:** Handle 70% of volume
   - Multiple partners per country
   - Redundancy if partner fails
   - Volume discounts through Lazada negotiation

3. **Regional Optimization:**
   - Indonesia: 17K islands (select major islands)
   - Philippines: 7K islands (similar strategy)
   - Thailand: Bangkok-centric initially, expand
   - Vietnam: North-South rural delivery challenges

## Cost Structure
- Urban delivery: $2-3 per order
- Rural delivery: $5-8 per order
- Island delivery: $10-15 per order
- Blended average: $4 per order

## Pricing Strategy
- Flat shipping: $5 for all areas
- Platform subsidy: $1-2 per order
- Seller covers: $3-4 per order
- Justification: Seller gets customer reach

## Results
- Coverage: 95% of population within 3-5 days
- Cost: Sustainable at current pricing
- Reliability: 95%+ on-time delivery
- Competitive: vs Shopee (similar logistics model)
