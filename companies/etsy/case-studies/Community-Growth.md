# Etsy Case Study: Community-Driven Growth

## Challenge
Scale 7M+ sellers without proportional support team growth
Support cost ratio critical to profitability

## Solution: Peer-to-Peer Community
- Forums: By category (jewelry, printables, vintage, etc.)
- Teams: 2M+ seller teams formed organically
- Mentorship: Experienced sellers help newcomers
- Knowledge base: User-generated guides and tips

## Results
- Support costs: Down 30% (peer support replaces tier-1)
- Seller satisfaction: 90%+ (community = belonging)
- Retention: Community members stay 3x longer
- Organic growth: Community = organic acquisition channel

## Scale Impact
- 7M sellers supported
- Support team: 1000 people (vs 3000 without community)
- Cost savings: $50M+ annually
- Compounding: Each new seller recruited via existing community
