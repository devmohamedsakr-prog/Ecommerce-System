# Etsy Challenge: Handmade Verification at Scale

## Problem
100M+ listings to verify
Counterfeiters selling mass-produced items as handmade
User trust critical to platform value

## Solution: Multi-Layer Verification
1. **AI Detection Phase:**
   - Reverse image search (detect stock photos/copied images)
   - Duplicate detection (same photo across multiple listings)
   - Seller pattern analysis (ship from China vs USA)

2. **Manual Review Phase:**
   - 200+ fulltime reviewers
   - Focus on high-value items (>$500)
   - Flagged items by AI (20% of new listings)
   - Seller history (repeat offenders flagged)

3. **Post-Sale Actions:**
   - Buyer reports (INAD - item not as described)
   - Automatic full refund
   - Seller suspension review
   - Repeat offenders: Permanent ban

## Scale Handling
- New listings daily: 1M+
- AI review: 20% flagged (~200K items)
- Manual review capacity: 5K items/day
- Backlog handled: Lowest priority auto-approved after 30 days

## Results
- 99%+ handmade authenticity maintained
- 100M+ listings verified
- Buyer trust: 95%+ confidence in handmade claim
- Seller reputation: Premium for verified handmade
