# Etsy System Architecture

**7M+ sellers | 100M+ listings | 200M+ monthly visitors**

## Handmade Verification
- AI detection: Reverse image search, duplicate detection
- Manual review: 200+ reviewers
- Seller history: Check for repeat violations
- Result: 99%+ handmade authenticity

## Seller Shop Platform
- Custom URL: etsy.com/shop/seller-name
- Analytics: Traffic, conversion rate, keywords
- Inventory management: Batch uploads
- Email tools: Seller communication

## Search & Discovery
- Elasticsearch: 100M+ indexed items
- ML recommendations: Item-to-item, user personalization
- Trending section: Real-time trending items
- Curation: Manual selection of featured collections

## Community Infrastructure
- Seller forums: Peer support (reduces support cost)
- Teams: By niche (jewelry, printables, etc.)
- Education: Webinars, guides, onboarding

## Payment & Shipping
- Etsy Payments: Unified processing
- Shipping labels: Discounted USPS, UPS, FedEx
- Seller payout: Daily deposits available
- Insurance: Integrated shipping insurance
