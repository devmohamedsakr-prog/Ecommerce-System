# Etsy Seller Onboarding Workflow

1. **Sign Up:** Email, password, basic info
2. **Verification:** ID check, payment method
3. **Shop Setup:** Shop name, URL, policies, bio
4. **First Listing:** Create, add photos, pricing, shipping
5. **Payment Setup:** Bank account for payouts
6. **Shop Launch:** Go live, shop visible to buyers
7. **Education:** Recommended courses, seller guides
8. **Community:** Join seller forums, teams

**Typical time:** 30 minutes to first listing
**Support:** Built-in help, community peer support
